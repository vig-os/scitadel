# [PR 304](https://github.com/vig-os/scitadel/pull/304) ci(vhs): parallelise the setup, and stop patching shards one at a time

ci(vhs): parallelise the setup, and lift the ceiling to 20 minutes

Fourth pass at #274, and the first with the evidence to say which part is the
actual cause.

## What was measured, in order

1. **#302** — `tui-papers` (5 tapes) killed at 10m0s. Every step had succeeded.
2. **Bounding `cli` (7 tapes) at 14m** — it was killed at 10m15s anyway, because
   the 14m never reached it: the ceiling was still 10m.
3. **`connectivity` — two tapes — killed at 10m16s** on the same run. Two tapes
   cannot need ten minutes, so **tape count was never the variable**.
4. Lifting everything to 20m fixed 8 of 10 shards, and `cli` rendered **two**
   of seven tapes in 20m16s — the setup+build step alone consumed ~20 minutes.

## What the log actually showed

    Install ffmpeg + ttyd, and build scitadel ... 17:16:39
    Run shard tapes                            17:35:10   <- 18m of setup
    ... two tapes rendered ...
    The operation was canceled.

The fixed cost — `apt-get update`, `apt-get install ffmpeg ttyd`, and a release
`cargo build`, run **serially** — is 4-20 minutes depending on the runner, and it
cannot be predicted from the tape count. 18 minutes of it on that job.

## The change

**Parallelise the two independent setup steps.** They share nothing, so the wall
clock is roughly the slower of the two instead of their sum. Measured: the small
shards dropped from ~9m to 3-4m.

**Lift the ceiling to 20m** as a net. It is a net, not the fix — a genuinely
hung tape now takes 20m to notice rather than 10, and that is the honest trade.

## What I tried and reverted, because it made things worse

I split every shard to <=3 tapes, on the reasoning that shard size was the
variable. It was not. With ten shards, `tui-launch` — **two tapes** — went from
passing in 3m33s to being killed at 20m8s, and the pre-existing `cli` still
consumed its whole budget. More shards means more concurrent setup on a
contended fleet, which made *every* job slower. The shard count is back to six.

## What is still not fixed

`cli` (7 tapes) and `tui-papers` (5) are the two largest shards and will still
exceed the ceiling on a slow runner. They need one of three things, and none is
mine to choose:

- a base image with ffmpeg and ttyd preinstalled, so the setup is a cache hit
  rather than an apt transaction;
- the rust cache actually hitting (its variance is the dominant term);
- fewer tapes, which costs a PR to rebalance and does not fix the contention.

Recorded on the budget field so the next person does not read 20 as the answer.
