# [PR 251](https://github.com/vig-os/scitadel/pull/251) docs(adr): ADR-007 literature acquisition — works/artefacts, route ladder, pacer, access boundary

Step 1 of the #230 + #234 plan: the design every acquisition slice builds on. `docs/decisions/ADR-007-2026-09-30-literature-acquisition.md`.

- **Data model (migration 013):**
  - `blobs` (content-addressed) separate from `artefacts`, with `UNIQUE(paper_id, kind, version, locator)`;
  - `acquisition_state` / `_attempts` / `_leases`, identity-check history, the pacer ledger, `tdm_authorisations`;
  - the DB is the source of truth, and `manifest.json` is a mirror;
  - a legacy backfill plus a flat-layout importer for consumer-written files.
- **One status vocabulary** backing coverage, `action_list` and `auth_pending`, including `not_entitled`, `wrong_version` and `needs_authorisation`.
- **Resolve, then rank** by version and licence (OA VoR > AM > preprint). Routes are corrected against 2026-09 reality: PMC `oa.fcgi` has been retired and replaced by the `pmc-oa-opendata` S3 dataset; Europe PMC is gated on `isOpenAccess`; SI comes from DataCite `IsSupplementTo`.
- **An unbypassable pacer:** routes can't reach `reqwest`, redirects are followed manually with a permit per hop, and buckets are per publisher platform, over a rolling 24 h window, ledgered in SQLite with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` and failing closed.
- **Access boundary:**
  - tier 2 is publisher-sanctioned TDM only, and there is no generic institutional-IP tier;
  - tier 4 runs in a **dedicated browser profile**, over a pipe, with a per-publisher origin allowlist;
  - it ships **one-click first**, and bulk draining is gated on a recorded per-publisher `tdm_authorisation`;
  - agents never supply URLs or paths, and never trigger browser fetches.
- **Slice plan:** S1–S7 with dependencies. raid's fastest path is S1 → S2 → (S3 ∥ S4). Prerequisite bugs: #248, #249, #250.

**Verified:**
- the migration 013 SQL applies cleanly on top of 001–012, and the integrity and foreign-key checks pass;
- lease upsert semantics (claim, refusal while live, takeover after expiry) and the artefact UNIQUE key were tested in SQLite;
- the `Route` / `RouteOutcome` / `Pacer` / `PacedClient` shapes compile as a `Vec<Arc<dyn Route>>` ladder.

**Also:** adds `vor` (Crossref's version-of-record value) to `.typos.toml`.

The design comes from five independent reviews plus the maintainer's decisions; both are recorded on #247.
